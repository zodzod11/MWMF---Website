# Scope of Services — Business Development Partnership

**Zachary Dieujuste ("ZD") & Renaissance Group, INC ("RG")**

**Date:** __________________

---

## 1. Purpose

This agreement establishes a mutual business development relationship between ZD and RG. Both parties agree to identify, develop, and advance business opportunities for one another's mutual benefit.

---

## 2. RG Deliverables

RG will provide the following services to ZD:

- **Opportunity Identification** — utilizing RG's professional relationships, network, market knowledge, and outreach capabilities to identify potential clients, partners, contracts, projects, transactions, and other business opportunities aligned with ZD's capabilities and interests.
- **Introductions & Relationship Development** — facilitating introductions between ZD and prospective clients, organizations, businesses, investors, partners, and other relevant parties, and supporting the development of those relationships.
- **Deal Development Support** — assisting with initial discussions, opportunity positioning, coordination with prospective parties, and strategic guidance related to advancing potential opportunities.
- **Strategic Partnerships** — identifying and pursuing partnership and collaboration opportunities that may create additional business opportunities for ZD.
- **Opportunity Tracking** — maintaining ongoing focus on active opportunities and supporting follow-up and coordination efforts.

---

## 3. ZD Deliverables

ZD will provide the following:

- **Professional Expertise & Execution** — subject matter expertise, deal evaluation, project execution, and operational capability necessary to advance identified opportunities.
- **Network Access** — ZD's professional relationships, market knowledge, and network connections that may be valuable to RG.
- **Opportunity Evaluation** — assessment of opportunities identified by RG to determine viability and fit.
- **Mutual Opportunity Identification** — identification of opportunities that may be valuable to RG or RG's network.
- **Intermediary Services** — for ZD-sourced third-party opportunities, ZD will serve as an ongoing intermediary, including facilitating communications, maintaining relationships with involved parties, providing status updates, and supporting deal advancement through the active development period. For ZD-affiliated entities, ZD's intermediary role is performed as a principal of the entity.

---

## 4. Mutual Compensation

### 4a. Success Fee (Both Directions)

Both parties agree to the following compensation structure:

| Scenario | Fee |
|---|---|
| RG introduces a qualifying opportunity that closes | ZD pays RG **3%** of gross value, **capped at $10,000** per opportunity |
| ZD introduces a qualifying opportunity that closes | RG pays ZD **3%** of gross value, **capped at $10,000** per opportunity |

**Qualifying Opportunity** means a business opportunity that:
- Is explicitly agreed to in writing by both parties **prior** to pursuit (including via Schedule A or a contemporaneous written notice)
- Is directly introduced by the introducing party
- Results in a closed transaction, contract, or revenue-generating engagement

**Presumption of Qualification:** An introduction to the same counterparty or concerning the same asset/project as the introduced opportunity is presumptively qualifying. The receiving party may object in writing within 10 business days of the introduction; otherwise the opportunity is deemed qualifying.

**Gross Value** means the total fees, commissions, profit share, or other compensation actually received by the paying party from the closed engagement — not the total transaction price, asset value, contract value, or funds raised. For revenue-generating engagements paid over time, the 3% applies to each payment received until the $10,000 cap per opportunity is reached.

**Property Transactions:** Notwithstanding the fee table above, for any real estate or property transaction within the term of this agreement where the other party provides a qualifying introduction of a buyer, seller, or lender, the success fee shall be **15% of the paying party's net commission** from the transaction. This rate applies to all property transactions during the term, with compensation due at the close of the transaction. The $10,000 per-opportunity cap does not apply to property transactions unless otherwise agreed in writing.

A **qualifying buyer introduction** means an introduction of a buyer who (a) executes a purchase and sale agreement (P&S) on the applicable property, or (b) enters into a written buyer-representation or agency agreement with the paying party's brokerage with respect to that property. A qualifying seller or lender introduction means an introduction that results in a written agreement with the paying party or a signed transaction document with the introduced seller or lender.

**Net Commission** means the paying party's commission actually received from the transaction, less the brokerage split owed to the paying party's brokerage. No deduction shall be made for desk fees, transaction fees, administrative fees, or any other internal or broker-imposed charges.

### 4b. Retainer for ZD-Sourced Third-Party Opportunities (Intermediary Services)

When ZD brings a qualifying opportunity to RG **where ZD is a pure intermediary** (not a principal, owner, board member, or employee of the counterparty entity), RG shall pay ZD a **$1,000 retainer** within 15 days of the **execution of a separate scope of services between RG and the opportunity's counterparty**. The retainer is **non-refundable**.

The retainer does not apply to:
- **Property transactions** — these are fee-only at close under §4a Property Transactions
- **ZD-owned or ZD-affiliated entities** (including but not limited to Turbo Construction and PPD Nonprofit) — these are zero-retainer deals where ZD's intermediary role is performed as a principal

The success fee on retainer-eligible ZD-sourced opportunities is calculated as follows:
- If 3% of gross value is **less than $1,000**: no additional fee is due (the retainer covers it)
- If 3% of gross value is **$1,000 or more**: the $1,000 retainer is credited against the success fee, and the remaining balance is due on close

**Example:** A ZD-sourced third-party deal closes at $30,000 gross value. 3% = $900. Since $900 < $1,000, no additional fee is due. The $1,000 retainer already paid is the full compensation.

This retainer applies only to ZD-sourced third-party opportunities. It does not apply to RG-sourced opportunities.

### 4c. Retainer for RG-Sourced Opportunities (Mirror Provision)

When RG brings a qualifying non-property opportunity to ZD, ZD shall pay RG a **$500 retainer** within 15 days of the opportunity being agreed to in writing under Section 4a. The retainer is **non-refundable** and is credited against the 3% success fee ZD owes RG on that opportunity upon close.

This mirror retainer does not apply to:
- **Property transactions** — these are fee-only at close under §4a Property Transactions
- Opportunities already listed on Schedule A at signing

### 4d. Payment Terms

All fees due under this agreement shall be paid within **15 calendar days** of the triggering event (close of opportunity for success fees, execution of counterparty scope of services for retainers). Late payments shall accrue interest at **1.5% per month** (18% annually) on the outstanding balance.

---

## 5. Scope of Covered Opportunities

This agreement covers only those opportunities **explicitly agreed to in writing by both parties** prior to engagement, subject to the presumption of qualification in §4a and the derivative opportunities provisions in §6. A blanket obligation does not apply to all business activities of either party.

Both parties remain free to pursue business opportunities independently without obligation to the other.

---

## 6. Derivative Opportunities

If either party uses the other party's introduction, relationship, or confidential information (as defined in Section 12) to generate a business opportunity beyond the original qualifying opportunity, that separate opportunity shall be automatically deemed a qualifying opportunity under this agreement. Both parties agree to document such derivative opportunities in writing within 10 business days.

This section does not create an ownership interest in either party's business relationships, assets, or network. It captures only opportunities that are directly traceable to the other party's specific introduction or information under this agreement.

---

## 7. Sunset Clause

A fee obligation applies only to qualifying opportunities that close within the applicable window below:

| Deal Type | Sunset Window |
|---|---|
| Property transactions | **90 days** from date of introduction |
| Non-property opportunities where a written scope of services / agreement with the counterparty is executed | **180 days** from date of introduction |
| All other non-property opportunities | **90 days** from date of introduction |

The date of introduction is the date the opportunity is recorded in writing (Schedule A entry or written notice). Verbal mentions do not start any clock.

For opportunities listed on Schedule A as of the date of this agreement, the applicable window begins on the date this agreement is signed by both parties.

Both parties may mutually agree in writing to extend the applicable window for specific opportunities where active development is ongoing.

No fee shall be payable for any opportunity that closes after the applicable window following the termination of this agreement.

---

## 8. Term

- **Initial Term:** 6 months from the date of this agreement
- **Renewal:** By mutual written agreement only
- **No automatic renewal**

---

## 9. No Exclusivity

Neither party is obligated to bring all opportunities to the other. Both parties may work with other partners, clients, and advisors simultaneously.

---

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Fee obligations for qualifying opportunities introduced prior to termination shall survive for the applicable sunset window described in Section 7.

---

## 11. Independent Contractor

ZD is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or joint venture partner of RG. Neither party has authority to bind the other.

---

## 12. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of business information, deal terms, entity details, operational data, and strategic plans disclosed under this agreement. This obligation survives termination of this agreement for 24 months. Neither party shall use the other's confidential information for any purpose outside the scope of this agreement without prior written consent.

---

## 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Any dispute arising under this agreement shall first be submitted to mediation before a mutually agreed mediator. Either party may seek relief in small claims court for amounts under $7,000. Each party shall maintain reasonable records of their activities under this agreement and shall provide relevant documentation to verify fees upon written request of the other party.

---

## 14. Assignment

ZD may assign his rights and obligations under this agreement to a wholly-owned entity upon written notice to RG. RG may not assign this agreement without ZD's prior written consent.
