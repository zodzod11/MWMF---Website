## Resolution

**WHEREAS,** the Corporation has been duly organized under the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and has obtained an Employer Identification Number from the Internal Revenue Service; and

**WHEREAS,** the Corporation requires depository accounts for the purpose of receiving grant funds, paying expenses, and conducting its financial operations; and

**WHEREAS,** the Board of Directors wishes to authorize the establishment of banking relationships and designate the individuals authorized to act on behalf of the Corporation;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:**

---

### 1. Primary Banking Relationship Authorized

The Treasurer is authorized and directed to establish a depository account at **Eastern Bank** for the Corporation's primary operating account.

### 2. Secondary Banking Relationship Authorized (if needed)

If the Corporation requires a depository account before the Eastern Bank account can be opened, or if the Board later determines a secondary account is necessary, the Treasurer is authorized to open an account at **DCU (Digital Federal Credit Union)** or another institution approved by the Board.

### 3. Types of Accounts Authorized

The Treasurer may open the following accounts:

| Institution | Authorized Accounts |
|---|---|
| Eastern Bank | Charitable Checking (primary operating account) |
| Eastern Bank | Charitable Savings or Money Market (grant reserves) |
| DCU (if needed) | Business checking or savings (temporary) |

### 4. Authorized Signatories

The following individuals are hereby designated as authorized signatories on all accounts of the Corporation. Any **one (1)** signature shall be sufficient to bind the Corporation on checks, drafts, withdrawals, and other banking transactions.

| Name | Title | Signature Authority |
|---|---|---|
| _________________________ | Treasurer | Sole signature |
| _________________________ | President / Executive Director | Sole signature |
| _________________________ | Clerk / Board Member | Sole signature |

**Special condition:** For any single transaction exceeding **$[5,000]** , two signatures shall be required.

### 5. Deposit of Funds

All funds received by the Corporation, including grant proceeds, donations, and any other revenue, shall be deposited promptly into the Corporation's bank accounts and shall not be commingled with personal funds of any director, officer, or employee.

### 6. Indemnification

The Corporation shall indemnify and hold harmless each authorized signatory for actions taken in good faith and in the ordinary course of the Corporation's business pursuant to this resolution.

### 7. Binding Effect

The Secretary is directed to certify this resolution and provide certified copies to each financial institution with which the Corporation maintains accounts. The institutions may rely on this resolution until receiving written notice of revocation.

### 8. Ratification

All actions taken by the officers and directors to establish banking relationships prior to the date of this resolution are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved.
