# 1. Role of the Treasurer

In a small startup nonprofit, the Treasurer is not a passive oversight role. You are the person who:

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| **Opens and manages bank accounts** | Primary relationship with Eastern Bank. You're the person they recognize by name. |
| **Maintains the books** | Records all income and expenses. Reconciles bank statements monthly. |
| **Manages cash flow** | Knows exactly how much money is in the bank, what it's restricted for, and how long it will last. |
| **Reports to the board** | Presents a financial report at each board meeting. Answers questions about spending and solvency. |
| **Files annual returns** | Form 990-N (IRS), MA Annual Report (SEC), MA PC filing (AG's office). |
| **Manages grant finances** | Tracks each grant separately: received, spent, remaining, any restrictions. |
| **Processes payments** | Pays bills, reimburses expenses, processes payroll (if/when staff exists). |
| **Ensures controls exist** | Two-signature thresholds, expense policies, conflict of interest compliance. |
| **Manages the credit card** | Primary cardholder. Reconcilies monthly. Ensures no personal charges. |
| **Leads the budget** | Drafts the annual budget. Presents it for board approval. Tracks actual vs budget. |

# 2. What You Own (and What You Don't)

| You Own | Colette (President/ED) Owns |
|---|---|
| Bank accounts & banking relationship | Program decisions & grant writing |
| Books & accounting system | Program spending decisions |
| Financial reporting to board | Fundraising & grant strategy |
| Tax & state filings | Community relationships & partnerships |
| Internal controls & policies | Staff/volunteer supervision |
| Budget process | Mission & program design |
| Grant tracking & compliance | Public-facing communications |

**Boundary:** You don't decide what to spend money on. Colette and the board decide. You make sure the spending happens correctly, is recorded, and stays within the budget the board approved.

# 4. Your Recurring Rhythm

## Weekly (10–15 minutes)

| Task | Detail |
|---|---|
| ✅ Log into Eastern Bank — scan account activity, check for unauthorized transactions |
| ✅ Check Capital One card balance — confirm no fraud |
| ✅ Note any large deposits or unusual activity |
| ✅ QuickBooks: snap photo of any receipts in your pocket |

## Monthly (45–60 minutes)

| Task | Detail |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Reconcile** bank statement against QuickBooks (or your ledger) |
| ✅ **Reconcile** credit card statement — match every charge to a receipt |
| ✅ **Categorize** any uncategorized transactions |
| ✅ **Pay credit card** in full. Never carry a balance. |
| ✅ **Update grant tracker** — add any new deposits, deduct any spending per grant |
| ✅ **Run financial statement** — check cash position, unrestricted vs restricted funds |
| ✅ **Pay any outstanding bills** — don't let them pile up |

## Quarterly (1–2 hours)

| Task | Detail |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Board financial report** — prepare and present (see Section 7 for template) |
| ✅ **Budget vs actual** — compare YTD spending against the annual budget |
| ✅ **Grant compliance check** — are you spending restricted funds correctly? |
| ✅ **Insurance review** — confirm D&O and liability policies are current |
| ✅ **Accounts receivable check** — any grant payments you're expecting that are late? |

## Annually (4–8 hours, spread across the year)

| Month | Task |
|---|---|
| **By Jan 31** | Prepare annual budget for board approval (if fiscal year = calendar) |
| **By Feb 28** | Compile prior year's financial records for 990 prep |
| **By May 15** | File **Form 990-N** (e-Postcard) with IRS — ~10 minutes online |
| **By incorporation anniversary** | File **MA Annual Report** (with Secretary of the Commonwealth) |
| **Within 30 days of 501(c)(3)** | File **PC designation** with MA AG's Non-Profits Division |
| **Year-round** | Annual report to grantors (if required by grant terms) |

# 5. Internal Controls & Policies to Adopt

These policies should be one page each, approved by the board, and filed with the minutes.

## 5.1 Expense Reimbursement Policy

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Receipt required for every expense over $25 | Audit trail |
| Reimbursement must be approved by someone other than the person being reimbursed | Prevents self-dealing |
| Personal expenses never paid from org funds | Legal requirement |
| Reimbursements paid within 15 days of submission | Good practice |

## 5.2 Two-Signature Threshold

| Threshold | Rule |
|---|---|
| Up to $5,000 | Any one authorized signatory |
| Over $5,000 | Two authorized signatories required |
| Over $25,000 | Board vote required before spending |

## 5.3 Credit Card Use Policy

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Card used for org expenses only | Obvious |
| Receipts kept for every charge | Audit trail for grantors |
| Monthly reconciliation by Treasurer | Catches errors early |
| Card never loaned to anyone not authorized by board | Control |

## 5.4 Conflict of Interest Policy

Massachusetts nonprofits are required to have this. At minimum:

- Any director or officer with a financial interest in a transaction must disclose it
- The interested person cannot vote on the transaction
- Transactions with interested persons must be in the org's best interest and at fair market value
- Colette's future compensation must be approved by the disinterested board members only

## 5.5 Grant Management Policy

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Each grant tracked separately in grant tracker | Know what money is for what |
| Restricted funds spent only on allowed purposes | Legal obligation to grantor |
| Unexpended grant funds returned if grant requires it | Don't spend money that isn't yours |
| Grant reports filed on time | Keeps you eligible for future funding |

# 6. Grant Financial Management

This is the most important operational skill for the Treasurer of a grant-funded org.

## The Grant Tracker

Make this spreadsheet before you receive any money:

```
| Grantor         | Program | Type    | Amount | Received | Spent | Remaining | Allowable Costs     | Report Due |
|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|----------|-------|-----------|---------------------|------------|
| Cummings Fdtn   | GO      | Unrestr | $25K   | TBD      | $0    | $25K      | Any program expense | N/A        |
| MA DPH          | Nav     | Restr   | $50K   | TBD      | $0    | $50K      | Navigator salary    | Quarterly  |
```

Columns explained:

| Column | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| **Grantor** | Who gave the money |
| **Program** | Which of your programs it supports |
| **Type** | Restricted or unrestricted (this is critical) |
| **Amount** | Total grant award |
| **Received** | Date funds hit the bank |
| **Spent** | Cumulative spending against this grant |
| **Remaining** | Amount - Spent |
| **Allowable Costs** | What the grant allows you to spend on |
| **Report Due** | When the grantor expects a financial report |

## Restricted vs Unrestricted Funds

| Type | You Can Spend It On | You CANNOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Unrestricted** | Anything that advances the mission | n/a |
| **Restricted** | Only what the grant says | Spend it on anything else (this is illegal) |

**As Treasurer:** You must know, at all times, which money in the bank is restricted and which is unrestricted. If you have $10K in the bank but $8K of it is restricted for navigation services, you have $2K of operating flexibility — not $10K.

# 7. Working with the Board & Colette

## Your Relationship with Colette

| Dynamic | How It Works |
|---|---|
| She decides what to spend on | Programs, supplies, outreach, events |
| You decide how to spend it correctly | Which account, which grant, which category |
| You both report to the board | She reports on programs. You report on finances. |
| You say "no" when it matters | If a spending proposal violates a grant restriction, you flag it. Not optional. |

## Board Financial Report Template

Present this at every board meeting (or quarterly at minimum):

**Financial Report — [Date]**

```
1. Cash Position
   - Total bank balance: $[X]
   - Unrestricted: $[X]
   - Restricted: $[X]

2. Grant Tracker Summary
   - Total grants received: $[X]
   - Total grants spent: $[X]
   - Total remaining: $[X]

3. Budget vs Actual (YTD)
   - Budgeted expenses: $[X]
   - Actual expenses: $[X]
   - Variance: $[X] ($[X]% over/under)

4. Upcoming
   - Expected deposits: $[X] from [Grantor], due [Date]
   - Upcoming bills: $[X] for [Purpose]
   - Solvency runway: [X] months at current burn rate
```

# 9. Common Pitfalls (and How to Avoid Them)

| Pitfall | Avoidance |
|---|---|
| **Commingling funds** | Never. Any personal expense on the org card is a red flag. Any org money in your personal account is a violation. |
| **Not tracking restrictions** | If you spend restricted money on the wrong thing and a grantor audits you, you may have to repay it. From personal funds. |
| **Failing to file 990-N** | Three years of missed filings = automatic revocation of 501(c)(3). Start over from scratch. |
| **No board report for months** | The board can't govern what they can't see. If you don't report, you're flying blind. |
| **Paying Colette without board approval** | Her compensation must be approved by the disinterested board members and documented in minutes. |
| **No D&O insurance** | Board members can be personally liable. No one should serve on a board without it. |
| **Losing the EIN letter** | Banks and grantors will ask for it repeatedly. Keep a PDF in Google Drive forever. |
| **Paying yourself before the org has money** | Don't. Reimburse expenses only. No salary or stipend until the board approves and cash flow supports it. |
