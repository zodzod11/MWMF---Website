## 🚨 Critical Issue — Cummings Won't Fund Very New Orgs

Cummings Foundation's eligibility explicitly excludes **"relatively new entities."** An organization incorporated on Monday and applying 5 days later is almost certainly too new. This is the biggest risk in the current plan.

**Options to address this:**

### Option A: Apply anyway (low probability but possible)
- Submit the LOI on Sept 16 as a brand-new entity
- Be transparent about being new; frame it as urgent unmet need
- Risk: likely filtered out at staff review before reaching volunteers
- Worth doing if there's nothing to lose — but don't count on it

### Option B: Fiscal sponsorship (stronger path)
- Find an existing 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor that aligns with maternal health
- Apply as a fiscally sponsored project
- Cummings does consider fiscal sponsorships (requires MOU)
- Funds would be restricted to the sponsored project
- The fiscal sponsor's track record helps with the "new entity" concern
- Potential sponsors: local community foundation, women's health org, birthing justice org

### Option C: Apply through a partner org
- Identify an existing Greater Boston nonprofit that would co-apply
- They submit; funds pass through to the PPD work
- Legal/fiscal relationship must be documented

### Option D: Target $25K or less for Coaches program
- Cummings Coaches program helps small nonprofits (requesting ≤$25K/year) develop their LOI
- Could help offset the "new entity" concern with coaching support
- Still may be filtered for being too new, but worth exploring

## Phase 1: Board & Incorporation (Monday)

### What the board list needs to include

For Massachusetts incorporation as a public charity, you generally need:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Minimum board members** | At least 3 (MA law for public charities) |
| **Officers** | President, Treasurer, Clerk (can be board members) |
| **Conflict of interest** | Board must adopt a conflict-of-interest policy |
| **Independent members** | Need at least one unrelated/independent director (especially for Colette's compensation approval later) |

### Suggested board composition for a fast start

1. **Colette Dieujuste** — Founder, President/Board Chair
2. **Zack** — Treasurer or Clerk (ops/finance/business systems)
3. **Independent member** — someone with nonprofit/healthcare/finance background, unrelated to Colette or Zack

> If you want to add more later, you can expand the board after incorporation. Start with the minimum to get the entity created.

### Incorporation steps (Massachusetts)

1. File **Articles of Organization** with the Secretary of the Commonwealth (online — can be done same-day)
2. File for **501(c)(3) tax exemption** (Form 1023 or 1023-EZ) — this takes weeks/months. For immediate grant eligibility, fiscal sponsorship may be needed.
3. Adopt **bylaws** (template available from MA nonprofit resources)
4. Hold **organizational meeting** — elect officers, adopt bylaws, approve initial actions
5. Get **EIN** from IRS (can be done online same day)

> ⚠️ 501(c)(3) determination from IRS takes 2–6 months (Form 1023-EZ) or 6–12 months (Form 1023). Without it, you need fiscal sponsorship for most grants.
