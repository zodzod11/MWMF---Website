# Renaissance Group — Scope of Work & Deliverable Checklist

**Client:** Women Maternal Wellness Foundation, Inc.  
**Consultant:** Renaissance Group / Paris Swindle  
**Retainer:** $4,500 (fixed) + 3% of grants secured  
**Version:** 1.0 — Proposed for engagement agreement  

---

## Purpose of This Document

This checklist defines every document and deliverable Renaissance Group will produce under the $4,500 fixed retainer portion of the engagement. It ensures both parties share the same understanding of scope before work begins.

**Retainer scope =** Entity formation, governance infrastructure, tax-exempt readiness, financial policies, compliance setup, and strategic positioning documents.

**3% scope =** Grant writing, submission, and follow-through (Cummings LOI + future applications).

*Items marked with a ★ are legally required for a Massachusetts nonprofit to exist and operate lawfully.*
